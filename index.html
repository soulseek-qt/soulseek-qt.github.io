What you can do

SoulseekQt keeps the useful parts close at hand: searching, sharing, transfers, conversations, and the people who make the network interesting.

⌕

Search deeply

Search the network for specific files, names, formats, folders, artists, albums, or whatever you are trying to track down.

↗

Share your collection

Choose folders to share and let other users browse the material you have decided to make available.

⇄

Manage transfers

Keep downloads and uploads visible, organized, and under your control while files move between users.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

What is SoulseekQt?

SoulseekQt is the official desktop client for the Soulseek peer-to-peer file-sharing network. It provides searching, sharing, downloads, uploads, user browsing, wishlists, chat, and rooms.

Is SoulseekQt free?

Yes. SoulseekQt is free to download and use. The Soulseek service is supported by voluntary donations.

Which operating systems are supported?

Official builds are provided for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

What files can I share?

Only share material you are legally allowed to share or have permission to distribute. Soulseek's rules prohibit unauthorized and unlawful uses of the service.

Does SoulseekQt contain advertising?

No. Soulseek states that the service is free and does not show advertising or include bundled software.

Where can I find official downloads?

The official Soulseek download page lists current builds and mirrors for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
