Timer fixes across international locales

A SoulseekQt maintenance release fixed timer behavior that could be affected by international regional settings on Windows and macOS.

The June 2024 release followed reports that several timers involved in peer messaging and file transfers could malfunction with particular international regions and calendars.

The issue was identified through user reporting and testing, then addressed in new builds for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

A practical maintenance release

Changes like this are easy to overlook because they are not visible features. They matter when regional settings, dates, and transfer behavior intersect, especially on a client designed for users around the world.
